HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Yipee! You've just scored a free delicious, fluffy, warm yorkshire pudding from the university! You're on your way back to point B, yorkshire pudding in hand, before you're brutally accosted by some ducks that have an eye for that pudding. Do your best to not have all your pudding eaten.

Controls

KeyAction
W / A / S / DMove the goose
QWear goose disguise
EHonk the horn to disperse geese

Mechanics

Player starts with 100 food, with each bite takig 10 Airhorn scared away geese within 300 units and has a 2 sec duration Goose disguise make the geese ignore the player for a 10 second duration 1st hidden event: Long Boi, is faster than regular geese and is not affected by air horn or geese disguise 2nd hidden event: Fox, targets the closest goose or Long Boi and disperses them
